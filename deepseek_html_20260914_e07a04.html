Who We Are

Professional Education
with Purpose.

The Institute of Climate Change and Health Research is Liberia's dedicated training institute for public health practitioners, climate researchers, and data scientists — equipping professionals with real-world skills to tackle the nation's most pressing challenges.

Our learning environment connects climate, health, environment, research, technology and professional practice. This collection brings together professionally prepared learning resources that support that integrated approach.
